# CrystlQuant

Free sports forecasts, graded in public.

Every game gets three numbers: CrystlQuant's win chance, a blind model that never sees the betting market, and the market itself. After the result, every call is graded on a public record, misses included.

- Today's board: https://crystlquant.com
- The record: https://crystlquant.com/record
- Our worst calls, on purpose: https://crystlquant.com/misses
- How the models work: https://crystlquant.com/method
- Use it in Claude, ChatGPT or your browser: https://crystlquant.com/tools
